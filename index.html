About Us

Disciplined Success is ever lasting

VTC is dedicated to providing quality education for students at feasible cost. We undertake coaching for students from 7th to 12th std for all subjects.

Discipline is one of our important mottos. It is necessary to be disciplined in order to achieve our goals in life, and the learning place is where young minds can learn and practise this. Apart from the education we impart, we also teach our students to value sincerity, punctuality and patience.

We don’t succeed unless our students succeed.

Our Aim & Focus

  • VTC helps students gain certainty and confidence to reach their potential by achieving the best grades in their tests.
  • We believe that learning a subject from different points of view helps a student fully understand the topic.
  • Our ethos is to recognise the potential in each student and give the highest quality of instruction to help them succeed.

Rules & Instructions

Discipline is part of our learning

For students
  • Attend all special classes and assessments (unit test, assigned test, revision exam, model exam) without fail.
  • Come to tuition classes on time (usually 6 PM).
  • Mobile phones, watches or any electronic items are not allowed inside the Tuition Centre.
  • Maintain the proper dress code as instructed. Full pants are a must for boys.
  • Hair style must be very decent. No funky or long hair styles for boys.
  • No fancy chains, lockets and rings. Do not carry cash beyond what is needed for travel.
  • Do not bring any motor vehicle without a driving licence.
  • Any public nuisance, malpractice, misbehaviour or disrespect during events may result in cancellation of admission.
  • Absence from exams without intimation will be fined.
For parents
  • Please inform the centre in advance about leave or late arrival of your ward.
  • Absence from classes without intimation will be viewed seriously.
  • Please discuss all tuition-related and ward-related issues only inside the office room.
  • Parents are not allowed to meet their wards during tuition hours.
  • Parents can meet teachers only between 5:30 – 6 PM and 8:30 – 9 PM.
  • Please meet the Class Incharge / Centre Head at least once a month by prior appointment. Attending all parent-teacher meetings is a must.
